Our services

Services

Scope of work

  • 01

    Model coordination

    We bring the architect, engineers and contractor into one shared 3D model and flag conflicts before they become problems on site.

    • ›One shared model for the whole team
    • ›A running list of issues to resolve
    • ›Weekly updates everyone can use
  • 02

    Drawing production

    We produce plans, sections and schedules from the model, so the drawings match the building being designed.

    • ›Plans, sections and details from the model
    • ›Files other teams can open
    • ›Drawings updated when the design changes
  • 03

    Scan to BIM

    We scan an existing building and turn it into an accurate digital model for renovation or to check the design against what is really there.

    • ›A scan of the existing building
    • ›A usable model of what is already built
    • ›A check of the design against the real building
  • 04

    BIM setup

    We set clear rules, file names and responsibilities, then stay with your team until the process works without constant help.

    • ›Rules everyone can follow
    • ›Clear roles for each person
    • ›Support on the first live project

How we work

From assessment to a working BIM solution

  1. 01 — Assess

    We review your models, workflows and project processes to identify gaps, risks and opportunities for improvement.

  2. 02 — Plan

    We define the right BIM standards, workflows, information requirements and project setup for your team.

  3. 03 — Deliver

    We implement the agreed workflows, coordinate models, support your team and leave you with a working solution.
